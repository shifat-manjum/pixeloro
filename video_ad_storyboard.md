# Pixeloro High-Converting Video Ad Storyboard & Production Blueprint

This video is specifically crafted for **Instagram Reels, TikTok, and Meta Video Ads (9:16 vertical format)**. It tells an emotional and relatable story that grips restaurant owners within the first 3 seconds and leads to an irresistible call-to-action.

---

## The Core Narrative & Story Arc

* **The Problem (0:00 - 0:05):** A hungry couple is standing outside a trattoria on a Friday evening. They pull out a phone, but the restaurant's menu is an impossible-to-read, slow-loading PDF. Frustrated, they walk away to another restaurant down the street.
* **The Solution (0:05 - 0:12):** Cut to another diner using a sleek, gold-accented mobile website built by Pixeloro. In 1 second, they view mouth-watering dishes and tap a glowing green button: **"Prenota su WhatsApp"**. Table booked instantly.
* **The Unbeatable Offer (0:12 - 0:20):** Pixeloro builds your custom restaurant website **100% FREE in 48 hours (€0 upfront)**. If you love it, it’s just **€55/month** all-inclusive.

## Ready-to-Use Video Assembly in 3 Minutes

1. **Images & Clips:** The 3 photorealistic vertical scene frames generated above provide the exact visuals for Scene 1, Scene 2, and Scene 3.
2. **CapCut / Canva Setup:**
   - Open CapCut or Canva (both free).
   - Create a **9:16 Story/Reel** project.
   - Place Scene 1 (0–5s), Scene 2 (5–13s), and Scene 3 (13–20s).
   - Add a subtle zoom/pan effect (Ken Burns effect).
   - Click **Auto-Captions** to add stylish animated subtitles in yellow and white.
3. **Audio Voiceover:**
   - Go to [ElevenLabs.io](https://elevenlabs.io) (free).
   - Paste the Italian or English script from above.
   - Choose a warm, authentic voice (like "Marco" or "Giovanni" for Italian, or "Adam" for English).
   - Add a low-volume lo-fi or upbeat acoustic guitar background track.
